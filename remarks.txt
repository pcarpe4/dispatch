Quarterly Financial Results — Prepared Remarks

Thank you, and good morning, everyone. I appreciate you joining us today, and I want to thank our shareholders, partners, and dedicated employees for their continued trust and support.

I am pleased to report that we delivered another strong quarter, reflecting the resilience of our business model and the disciplined execution of our long-term strategy. Our results underscore the fundamental strength of our franchise and our unwavering commitment to creating sustainable, long-term value for all of our stakeholders.

Revenue for the quarter came in ahead of expectations, driven by robust demand across our core segments and continued momentum in our key growth markets. We saw healthy top-line growth on both a year-over-year and sequential basis, supported by strong customer engagement, expanding wallet share, and the successful execution of our go-to-market initiatives.

Importantly, this growth was both broad-based and high-quality. We continued to diversify our revenue streams, deepen our relationships with existing customers, and win new business in strategically important verticals. Our recurring revenue base remained a source of stability and predictability, providing a solid foundation for the quarters ahead.

Turning to profitability — we delivered meaningful margin expansion this quarter, a direct result of our ongoing focus on operational efficiency, cost discipline, and prudent capital allocation. Gross margins improved as we benefited from favorable mix, pricing power, and economies of scale. Operating margins also expanded as we continued to drive productivity gains while making targeted investments in the areas that matter most to our future.

Our balance sheet remains exceptionally strong. We continue to generate healthy free cash flow, which affords us tremendous flexibility to invest organically, pursue accretive opportunities, and return capital to shareholders. During the quarter, we returned a significant amount of capital through our dividend and share repurchase programs, reflecting our confidence in the durability of our cash flows and our commitment to a balanced and shareholder-friendly capital allocation framework.

We also continued to invest thoughtfully in innovation, talent, and technology — the engines of our long-term growth. We believe these investments position us to capitalize on the substantial market opportunity ahead and to extend our competitive advantages over time.

Looking forward, while we remain mindful of the dynamic macroeconomic environment and the uncertainties that persist, we are confident in our ability to navigate near-term headwinds and emerge even stronger. We are taking a measured and disciplined approach, balancing prudent risk management with continued investment in our strategic priorities.

For the full year, we are reaffirming our guidance and remain confident in our outlook. We expect continued top-line growth, ongoing margin improvement, and strong cash generation. Our pipeline is healthy, our fundamentals are sound, and our team is executing at a high level.

In closing, I want to once again thank our employees around the world for their hard work, dedication, and commitment to excellence. None of these results would be possible without them. And to our shareholders, thank you for your continued confidence. We remain laser-focused on disciplined execution, sustainable growth, and the creation of long-term value.

With that, we look forward to taking your questions. Thank you.
